Welcome to the Rig Veda Explorer

The Rig Veda is the oldest of the four Vedas, a collection of ancient Sanskrit hymns composed between 1500 and 1000 BCE. It consists of 1,028 hymns (sūktas) organized into ten books (maṇḍalas).

This interactive visualization helps you explore the structure, themes, and deities of the Rig Veda in an engaging way.
